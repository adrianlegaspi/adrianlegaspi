## Context

FixedAI is a complete product that never shipped to the public. It is worth showing for
what it is: **finished engineering, not a launched business.** The distinction matters and
is stated plainly rather than hidden.

## The problem

The product addressed AI-assisted repair of a specific class of work that people were
doing by hand. The engineering question was whether the assisted path could be made
reliable enough to trust without review.

## What I did

- Designed and built the product end to end, alone.
- Took it to a genuinely complete state: onboarding, core workflow, error paths.
- Made the call not to launch rather than ship something that needed a market it did not have.

## Technical highlights

React and TypeScript front end over a Node.js API, with LLM calls constrained to typed
operations and validated before use. Building solo forces the useful discipline of
choosing boring, well-understood parts.

## Outcome

Complete and unreleased. Deciding not to launch is a real outcome, and a cheaper one than
the alternative.
