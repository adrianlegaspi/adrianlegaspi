# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, and prospective consulting clients evaluating Adrian for Senior Software Engineer or Tech Lead-capable work. They need to understand his experience and reach project evidence quickly, whether or not they engage with the 3D city.

## Product Purpose

Present Adrian's portfolio as a memorable low-poly city where projects are buildings, while keeping every case study available as accessible HTML. Success means visitors understand Adrian's seniority, professional experience, project work, and contact options within 10 to 15 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Playful, crafted, credible. The city should feel colorful and distinctive without becoming childish or game-like.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS presentation, dark hacker or cyberpunk aesthetics, photorealism, game mechanics, visual clutter, and effects that compete with case-study content.

## Design Principles

- Make the city memorable, but keep the portfolio immediately usable.
- Use the built-city metaphor to show engineering work instead of explaining it.
- Keep interactions obvious and lightweight for time-constrained visitors.
- Let case studies carry credibility once a project is selected.
- Prefer a finished, simple experience over ornamental complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep all content reachable through semantic DOM navigation and keyboard controls. Never make hover or WebGL the only route to information. Preserve visible focus, large touch targets, reduced-motion behavior, and a functional HTML fallback.
