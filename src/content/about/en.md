## About

More than nine years building software people use to do their jobs: internal platforms,
security tooling, fintech and operational applications. The kind of software that is
judged on whether it holds up, not on whether it demos well.

Currently the sole frontend engineer on a centralized internal security platform serving
more than 4,000 users at Viasat, embedded in a backend-heavy team of 15. Before that,
fintech features for a platform with 10,000 users, Angular dashboards and Laravel APIs
for more than 3,000 legal professionals, and a pre-hospital logging app used by 180+
firefighters and paramedics across 18 stations in Tijuana.

## What I do

- **Full-stack product engineering**: Node.js and TypeScript services, React front ends, PostgreSQL.
- **Architecture**: multi-tenant systems, data modelling, service boundaries that survive contact with new requirements.
- **Technical leadership**: tech lead for five engineers on an agentic HR platform, breaking work into shippable slices, code review as teaching.
- **AI integration**: retrieval, fine-tuning and LLM evaluation that run in production rather than in a demo.

## How I work

I prefer the smallest thing that actually works, and I would rather ship a finished simple
system than an elegant speculative one. Most of the value in senior work is in choosing
what not to build.

Outside of work I ship games, which keeps me close to the whole product cycle from concept
through store review.

## Availability

Open to Senior Software Engineer and Tech Lead roles, and to freelance or consulting work.
