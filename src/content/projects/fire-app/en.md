## Context

Early professional work: software built for the Tijuana Fire Department. It is the oldest
project in this city, and its building is not small because the work is old — this is
where the work started being real.

## The problem

Operational record keeping ran on paper and spreadsheets. Information that mattered during
an incident was slow to retrieve and easy to lose.

## What I did

- Built the application for the day-to-day record keeping of the department.
- Worked directly with the people using it, on their terms and their hardware.
- Delivered something usable under real constraints rather than ideal ones.

## Technical highlights

JavaScript and Node.js over MySQL — the pragmatic stack for the context and for the
maintenance story available at the time. The lasting lesson was about constraints:
software for emergency services is judged on whether it works when someone is in a hurry.

## Outcome

Delivered and used by the department. Exact scope, dates and public wording are being
confirmed before further detail is published.
