## Context

Built in 2018 for a restaurant owner who needed to manage daily operations and planned to
offer the service to other businesses.

## The problem

The restaurant needed a mobile tool for sales and inventory. The product also needed a
foundation that could grow into a service for other operators.

## What I did

- Built the Android client with Cordova.
- Developed point-of-sale and inventory workflows for employees.
- Built the PHP and MySQL backend that supported the mobile app.

## Technical highlights

Cordova delivered the mobile client while PHP and MySQL handled business data and the
application backend.

## Outcome

Development ended in 2020 when the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted the restaurant business.
The archived screenshots show the original app.
